Profile

My work spans AI enablement, strategic policy, futures, regulation and program delivery. I frame complex problems, bring together people who hold different parts of them and help turn discussion into practical work.

I began my government career in strategic policy after studying urban planning. Since then I have worked across regulation, emerging technology and organisational change. Repeated coordination problems led me towards futures, systems change and technology integration.

  • Problem framing and synthesis: Clarify the underlying problem and turn ambiguity into useful choices.
  • Strategic facilitation and convening: Design conversations that surface assumptions and help groups act.
  • Systems and futures thinking: Work across longer horizons while staying grounded in practical next steps.
  • Network and relationship building: Build and steward relationships across organisations and disciplines.
  • Practice-led experimentation: Use workshops and small interventions to test ideas in practice.

  • AI Enablement in a Box turns recurring lessons into reusable resources that agencies can adapt to their own operating environments.
