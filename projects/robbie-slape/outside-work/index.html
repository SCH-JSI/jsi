Outside Work

Outside work I spend a lot of time walking and hiking, taking photographs, looking at art and moving between cityscapes and natural landscapes. I am particularly drawn to liminal and in-between spaces. I notice edges, transitions and contrasts where one condition meets another. Photography is one way I practise paying attention to place, detail and change.
