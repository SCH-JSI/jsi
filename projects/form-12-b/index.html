About this game & your answers

Based on Creative Bureaucrats and Friends — Where are we going? and Robbie Slape’s farewell reflections. The game explores their questions; it does not prescribe a future or speak for the Hub.

All institutional and funding scenarios are hypothetical. Named organisations have not made offers in this game. July 2027 is the paper’s planning horizon, not an announced Hub event.

Your answers exist only in this tab’s memory. No login, database, analytics or answer transmission. Refreshing or starting over clears them. The web host receives ordinary page requests. Copying a profile is optional and puts only the displayed short summary on your clipboard.

Energy and time ranges are illustrative game assumptions, not validated estimates or commitments. This is a reflection prompt, not a personality test.
