Practical AI for Public Servants

This 60-page slide deck is the source resource behind the field guide. It was developed for the regular AI Colab workshop and introduces how large language models behave, where they are useful, how to delegate work clearly, and where human judgement must remain in charge.

The field guide turns the workshop material into searchable notes and practical tools. Use the original slides when you want the complete workshop sequence, examples and visual explanations.
