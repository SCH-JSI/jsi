The field guide

This field guide adapts Practical AI for Public Servants, the resource used in the regular workshop hosted at the AI Colab. It turns the workshop into short, reusable notes for public-sector work, with links to deeper research and current Australian Government policy.

UnderstandA useful working model: learned patterns, enormous scale, impressive reach - and limits that do not disappear when the prose sounds certain.
JudgeJudge the task before the tool. The key question is not “can it produce something?” but “what must be true for that something to be useful?”
WorkPrompting is closer to delegation than incantation: set direction, supply material, break work apart, inspect it and remain accountable.

Build a delegation brief

Choose the role, intent and output shape, then add the context that should govern the work. This tool arranges your instructions; it does not contact an AI system.

ROLE
Act as a careful Australian public-sector policy adviser. Prioritise administrative context, evidence and decision usefulness.

QUALITY BAR AND HUMAN REVIEW
[State acceptance criteria, required source pointers, approval owner and review gate.]

WORKING RULES
- Distinguish source evidence from inference.
- Cite or point to the supplied source for each material claim.
- Surface missing information, conflicts and uncertainty instead of guessing.
- Do not make decisions reserved for the accountable human officer.
